Privacy

Updated: October 16, 2026

This page explains data handling by the official Arcadium Client website, launcher and game client.

The website

Cloudflare Pages serves the website, and GitHub Releases hosts installer downloads. This site has no advertisements, visitor analytics or contact forms. The site does not require an account or store your launcher credentials.

Cloudflare and GitHub process connection information, such as IP addresses, to provide and protect their services. See their privacy policies for details.

Cloudflare Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement

Data on your computer

The launcher stores settings, game files and account credentials on your computer. Saved credentials use DPAPI for the current Windows user or Keychain on macOS. You can choose a storage directory during setup. Distributed installers contain no saved accounts.

Cookie login reads the selected file locally and uses its Microsoft session for sign-in. Microsoft may open in an isolated native window to confirm access. Imported cookies are not saved with your account or uploaded to Arcadium's badge service.

External services

Microsoft, Xbox and Mojang handle sign-in, ownership checks and profile retrieval. Sign-in takes place in your browser or an isolated native Microsoft window. Installing games, Java and mods contacts Mojang, Modrinth and the components' download providers. External services and additional mods have their own privacy policies.

Arcadium badges

While you are in a world, the client sends your Minecraft access token over HTTPS to Arcadium's badge service to verify your player UUID. The service verifies the token without saving it and stores temporary presence sessions containing your UUID, a hashed session token and an expiry time. The client exchanges UUIDs from the world and player list to show which players are using Arcadium. Cloudflare hosts this service and processes connection information to operate and protect it.

Online count

Versions that support online counting send a short heartbeat while the launcher or game is running. Each installation generates a random identifier and secret on your computer. The service stores hashes of those values and temporary session identifiers, with 90-second activity leases. A launcher and game from the same installation count once. This count receives no Minecraft account names, UUIDs or access tokens, and creates no activity history. Expired records are regularly removed.

The website receives only the total and its update time. Turn off Settings > Launcher > Online Count in the launcher to exclude both the launcher and game. Connection addresses are used for temporary abuse limits without being saved by the counting service. Cloudflare processes connection information to provide and protect the service.

Discord Rich Presence

When Discord RPC is enabled in the launcher, Arcadium sends its running activity, Minecraft version and elapsed time to Discord. You can manage this through the launcher setting and Discord's activity-sharing settings.

Controls and questions

Launcher data is managed in its local directory. Questions and bugs can be reported through our Discord. Do not include passwords, tokens or personal information in public posts.
