macOS

Open the DMG and drag Arcadium Client into Applications. The launcher prepares Java and your selected Minecraft version.

  • Apple Silicon: macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • Intel: macOS 12 Monterey or later
  • Minecraft Java Edition account for authenticated servers
  • Internet access for setup and sign-in

Find your processor in the Apple menu under About This Mac. An Apple M-series chip uses the Apple Silicon download. An Intel processor uses the Intel download.
