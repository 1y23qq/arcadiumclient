Choose your Mac.

Open the DMG and drag Arcadium Client into Applications. The launcher prepares Java and the game on first launch.

  • Intel: macOS 12 Monterey or later
  • Apple Silicon: macOS 13 Ventura or later
  • A purchased Minecraft Java Edition account
  • Internet access for setup and sign-in

Not sure which chip you have? Open the Apple menu, then About This Mac. Choose Apple Silicon for an Apple M-series chip, or Intel for an Intel processor.

Current release: 0.5.6 Beta.
