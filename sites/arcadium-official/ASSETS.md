# Website assets

`public/` contains the handwritten website source restored from stable Arcadium 0.3.6. No private account data is part of this directory.

The Arcadium A, launcher/client screenshots and `fps-comparison-original.jpg` were supplied or already owned by this project. The supplied JPEG remains byte-for-byte unchanged. Its left, center and right captures show Lunar Client at 241 FPS, Badlion Client at 269 FPS and Arcadium Client at 421 FPS. They are individual visible readouts, not timed averages.

The current screenshots were supplied by the owner on 2026-10-22 and are copied without modification:

- `launcher-home-f640f51643fe.png`: `launcher.png`, 1263 × 865.
- `client-main-menu-d39da5ba1e1d.png`: `main menu.png`, 1612 × 940.
- `client-mod-menu-1b4da1abc2e1.png`: `ingame mod menu.png`, 1920 × 1080.

Their filenames include the first 12 characters of each source file's SHA-256 hash for cache invalidation. Full hashes are recorded in [the screenshot provenance file](../../licenses/assets/site-screenshots-2026-10-22.json). They retain their original aspect ratios. The older Glass theme images are retained as historical assets and are no longer displayed.

The comparison SVG embeds that entire 1920 × 1080 JPEG below a separate header. It adds plain client names, the same readouts and the project's own Arcadium A. It does not reproduce standalone Lunar or Badlion logo assets, alter the game image or crop its counters. The clients' names identify the compared products and imply no endorsement.

The owner reported an Intel Core i7-13620H CPU, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU, 1920 × 1080 game resolution and an 8-chunk render distance. Other graphics settings and client versions were not supplied. The performance chart uses a shared zero-based 500 FPS scale and computes displayed percentages from those visible readouts. Arcadium bar animation follows the reduced-motion preference and pauses outside the viewport or when the page is hidden.
