# Forge 1.7.10 dependencies

These notices are copied from the exact official JARs pinned in `distribution/forge-1.7.10.dependencies.json`, or their matching source tags when a JAR omits its license text. Original paths, hashes, origins and source links are recorded in `dependencies.json`. The libraries remain under their original licenses and are downloaded unchanged. Arcadium integration source is in `client-forge1710`.

The launch profile and its library sources are maintained by [GTNewHorizons/lwjgl3ify](https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/lwjgl3ify/tree/3.0.39). Forge and FML notices are also retained in the parent directory. Mixin, ASM, Kotlin, LWJGL, SLF4J and Log4j retain their respective upstream licenses.
