# Bundled download dependency notices

`artifacts.json` records the exact SHA-1/SHA-256, original authors and license
declarations of the retained historical 0.3.6 external download dependencies.
`texts/` preserves complete license and notice documents, deduplicated by hash.
Nested and native library corroboration is also recorded under `../assets/`.

These projects retain their original licenses. Arcadium's project GPL license
does not relicense them. In particular, Argentum, Argentum Extras and Cera retain
their original reserved-rights terms and are obtained unchanged from their
official release endpoints. Their source and binaries are not published in this
repository or embedded in Arcadium's GPL components.

Recorded upstream source pins are identified separately from exact release
corroboration. An upstream source link is not a claim of a bitwise reproducible
build. The original license declarations and copyright notices remain unchanged.
